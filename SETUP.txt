MISHRA WEB STUDIO — ONE FILE EMAIL SETUP

1. Open index.html.
2. Search for YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY.
3. Create a Web3Forms access key at https://web3forms.com/
4. Paste your access key there.
5. Replace YOUR_BUSINESS_EMAIL@example.com with the email where you want enquiries.
6. Upload only index.html to your hosting.
7. Submit a test enquiry from another email/device.

The form sends the visitor's name, business, email, selected service, budget and project details to your configured business email through Web3Forms.

Do not put passwords, private keys, or other secrets in the HTML file.
