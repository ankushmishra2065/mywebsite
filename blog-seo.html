Website SEO basics for beginners

SEO starts with making your pages useful and understandable, not with stuffing keywords into every sentence.

Clear page titles

Give each important page a descriptive title and explain the topic naturally in the content.

Useful structure

Use headings, paragraphs, internal links and descriptive navigation so both people and search engines can understand the site.

Performance and mobile UX

A responsive, fast experience helps visitors find and use your content across devices.

Original value

Create content from your own experience, expertise and services rather than copying or lightly rewriting other sites.
