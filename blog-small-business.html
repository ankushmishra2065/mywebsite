Why every small business needs a website

A website gives a business a home it controls, a place to explain its services clearly and a destination that can be found through search.

1. Credibility

A clear website can answer basic questions before a customer contacts you: what you sell, where you operate, how to reach you and what makes your business different.

2. Search visibility

A well-structured site can create useful pages that search engines can understand. Local businesses can also connect their website with their wider online presence.

3. Conversion

Good pages guide visitors toward a useful next step such as calling, messaging, requesting a quote or visiting a store.
