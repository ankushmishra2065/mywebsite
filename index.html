What EduNexa is based on

Curriculum structure is aligned to current resources published by Nepal's Curriculum Development Centre (CDC). Content inside EduNexa is original study support, not a reproduction of textbooks.

Official CDC resources

CDC publishes Grade 11–12 curriculum and textbook resources, including Mathematics, English, Nepali, Social Studies, Accountancy and Business Studies. The site also lists updated/recent 2082 publications.
